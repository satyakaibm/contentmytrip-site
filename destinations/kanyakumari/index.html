Tamil Nadu, India

Kanyakumari

India's southernmost point, where three seas meet under one sunrise and one sunset.

Best time to visitOctober to March
Ideal duration1–2 days
Nearest airportThiruvananthapuram International Airport — about 85 km
Budget₹8,000–₹18,000 per person for 2 days
Typepilgrimage

Kanyakumari sits at India's southernmost tip, where the Arabian Sea, Bay of Bengal, and Indian Ocean meet — famous for its sunrise-sunset views and the Vivekananda Rock Memorial.

Experiences

  • Watch sunrise and sunset: See both from the same beach, a rarity thanks to Kanyakumari's southern-tip geography.
  • Ferry to Vivekananda Rock: Take the short ferry ride out to the memorial and Thiruvalluvar statue.
  • Browse the seashell market: Shop for shell crafts and souvenirs along the beachfront market street.
  • Visit Padmanabhapuram Palace: Tour the intricately carved wooden halls of this centuries-old royal palace.

Attractions

  • Vivekananda Rock Memorial: A memorial on a rock islet offshore, reachable by ferry, marking where Swami Vivekananda meditated.
  • Thiruvalluvar Statue: A 133-foot statue of the Tamil poet-saint standing beside the Vivekananda Rock.
  • Kanyakumari Beach: The confluence point of three seas, famous for its shared sunrise-sunset viewing spot.
  • Padmanabhapuram Palace: A well-preserved 16th-century wooden palace, about 30 minutes from town.

Local food

  • Kerala-Tamil Thali: A regional fusion thali reflecting the nearby Kerala and Tamil Nadu borders.
  • Fresh Seafood: Locally caught fish and prawns, grilled or curried, from the surrounding seas.
  • Filter Coffee: Strong South Indian coffee, a staple at every local eatery.
  • Banana Chips: Crisp fried banana slices, a popular South Indian snack.
