Odisha, India

Dhenkanal

A hill-ringed heritage town where a Shiva shrine crowns the peak and elephants roam the forest below.

Best time to visitOctober to March
Ideal duration2–3 days
Nearest airportBiju Patnaik International Airport, Bhubaneswar — about 75 km
Budget₹6,000–₹14,000 per person for 3 days
Typeheritage

Dhenkanal is an Odisha heritage town at the edge of the Eastern Ghats, home to the state's only fort-cum-palace, the hilltop Kapilash Shiva temple, and the Kapilash Wildlife Sanctuary — a key elephant corridor linking the region's forests.

Experiences

  • Climb Kapilash hill: Take the 1,352-step pilgrim stairway, or the hill road, up to the Shiva temple for panoramic views of the surrounding plains.
  • Spot elephants at Kapilash Sanctuary: Visit the elephant rescue centre and look out for wild elephants moving through this active forest corridor.
  • Trek Saptasajya's seven hills: Follow forest trails past waterfalls to the Raghunath Temple, especially lively during Ramanavami.
  • Tour the royal Rajbati: Walk the grounds of Odisha's only fort-cum-palace and learn about the royal family that once ruled Dhenkanal.

Attractions

  • Kapilash Temple: A 12th–13th century Shiva shrine atop Mount Kapilash, reached via 1,352 stone steps or a motorable hill road, prized for its Kalinga-style architecture and views.
  • Kapilash Wildlife Sanctuary: A forest sanctuary and vital elephant corridor linking Dhenkanal to Athagarh and Cuttack, with an elephant rescue centre caring for injured and orphaned elephants.
  • Dhenkanal Palace (Rajbati): Odisha's only fort-cum-palace, built by the local royal family in the late 19th century on the site of a fort that withstood Maratha sieges.
  • Saptasajya: A cluster of seven forested hills about 11 km from town, with the hilltop Raghunath Temple, seasonal waterfalls, and a small wildlife sanctuary.
  • Sarana Srikhetra Temple: Sarana Srikhetra, Dhenkanal — a beautiful Jagannath temple known for its peaceful spiritual atmosphere, where devotees can experience the essence of Jagannath culture and seek Mahaprabhu’s blessings.
  • Maa Ramachandi Temple Nadhara: Maa Ramachandi Temple, Dhenkanal — a peaceful spiritual destination where devotees visit to seek Maa Ramachandi’s blessings while experiencing the temple’s serene and devotional atmosphere.
  • Sapua Dam: Sapua Dam, Dhenkanal — a peaceful nature getaway surrounded by lush green hills and scenic water views, perfect for picnics, photography, and a relaxing day away from the city.

Local food

  • Dhenkanal Bara: A famous urad-dal fritter from the town, crisp outside and soft within — considered one of Odisha's best.
  • Chhena Poda: A caramelised baked cottage-cheese dessert enjoyed across Odisha.
  • Dalma: A comforting lentil-and-vegetable stew, lightly tempered with cumin, garlic and red chilli.
  • Pakhala Bhata: Fermented rice soaked in water and curd, served with fried fish, potato and papad — a staple Odia lunch.
