Abu Dhabi Emirate, United Arab Emirates

Abu Dhabi

The UAE's stately capital — grand mosques, island museums, and a quieter cousin to Dubai.

Best time to visitNovember to March
Ideal duration2–3 days
Nearest airportZayed International Airport — about 30 km from downtown
Budget₹45,000–₹1,00,000 per person for 3 days
Typecity

Abu Dhabi is the UAE's cultural capital, home to the Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque, the Louvre Abu Dhabi, Yas Island theme parks, and a scenic waterfront corniche.

Experiences

  • Sunset at the Corniche: Walk or cycle the 8km waterfront promenade as the skyline lights up at dusk.
  • Desert safari: Dune-bash into the desert outside the city for camel rides and a Bedouin-style dinner.
  • Ferrari World ride: Experience Formula Rossa, one of the world's fastest roller coasters, on Yas Island.
  • Saadiyat museum walk: Spend a morning in the Louvre Abu Dhabi and Saadiyat's cultural district.

Attractions

  • Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque: One of the world's largest mosques, with hand-knotted carpets and gleaming white marble domes.
  • Louvre Abu Dhabi: A landmark museum under Jean Nouvel's perforated dome, showing art across cultures and eras.
  • Qasr Al Watan: The UAE's presidential palace, open to visitors for its ornate halls and a nightly light show.
  • Yas Island: Home to Ferrari World, Yas Waterworld and Yas Marina Circuit, the emirate's entertainment hub.

Local food

  • Al Machboos: Spiced rice with meat or fish, the Emirati take on biryani.
  • Shawarma: Spit-roasted meat wrapped in flatbread, a UAE street-food staple.
  • Luqaimat: Deep-fried dumplings drizzled with date syrup.
  • Balaleet: Sweet saffron vermicelli served with an omelette, a traditional Emirati breakfast.
