Phuket Province, Thailand

Phuket

Thailand's biggest island, where jungle-backed beaches meet the buzz of Patong by night.

Best time to visitNovember to March
Ideal duration4–5 days
Nearest airportPhuket International Airport — about 30 km from Patong
Budget₹35,000–₹80,000 per person for 4 days
Typebeach

Phuket is Thailand's largest island, famous for its palm-fringed beaches, vibrant Patong nightlife, limestone cliffs, and easy boat access to the Phi Phi Islands.

Experiences

  • Island-hop to Phi Phi: Take a speedboat day trip to Phi Phi Don and Phi Phi Leh for snorkelling and Maya Bay.
  • Patong nightlife: Spend an evening on Bangla Road's bars, night markets and cabaret shows.
  • Explore Old Town: Walk the Sino-Portuguese shophouse streets and try local coffee and Thai desserts.
  • Sunset at a viewpoint: Catch sunset from Karon or Promthep Cape viewpoint over the Andaman Sea.

Attractions

  • Patong Beach: Phuket's liveliest beach strip, backed by Bangla Road's bars, clubs and night markets.
  • Big Buddha Phuket: A 45m marble-clad Buddha statue on Nakkerd Hill with panoramic island views.
  • Old Phuket Town: Sino-Portuguese shophouses, street art and heritage cafes in the island's historic quarter.
  • Phi Phi Islands: A dramatic limestone-cliff island group reachable by day-trip speedboat, including Maya Bay.

Local food

  • Pad Thai: Stir-fried rice noodles with egg, tofu/shrimp, peanuts and lime — Thailand's signature street dish.
  • Tom Yum Goong: A hot-and-sour shrimp soup with lemongrass, galangal and chilli.
  • Moo Hong: A Phuket-style braised pork belly dish, sweet and dark from slow-cooked soy and palm sugar.
  • Roti: A crisp fried pancake street snack, served sweet with banana and condensed milk or savoury.
