Karnataka, India

Bangalore

India's Garden City, where tech-park skylines meet leafy parks and a buzzing pub scene.

Best time to visitOctober to February
Ideal duration3–4 days
Nearest airportKempegowda International Airport, Bangalore
Budget₹5,000–₹15,000 per person for 3 days
Typecity

Bangalore (Bengaluru) is Karnataka's capital and India's tech capital — a green, high-altitude city of leafy boulevards, colonial-era gardens, and glass tech parks, famous for its year-round pleasant climate, craft breweries, and a food scene running from centuries.

Experiences

  • Pub-hop in Indiranagar: Bangalore is India's craft-beer capital — spend an evening moving between microbreweries and rooftop bars in Indiranagar or Koramangala.
  • Cycle or walk through Cubbon Park: Join the car-free Sunday mornings in Cubbon Park for a relaxed walk, run or cycle through the city's green core.
  • Shop on Commercial Street: Browse the narrow lanes of one of Bangalore's oldest shopping districts for clothes, jewellery and street food.
  • Day trip to Nandi Hills: Head out before dawn to Nandi Hills, about 60 km away, for a sunrise over the Deccan plateau.

Attractions

  • Lalbagh Botanical Garden: A 240-acre botanical garden laid out in the 18th century, home to a glasshouse modelled on London's Crystal Palace and a striking annual flower show.
  • Bangalore Palace: A Tudor-style royal palace built in 1887 for the Wadiyar family, with fortified towers, wood-carved interiors and manicured lawns.
  • Cubbon Park: A 300-acre green park in the heart of the city, ringed by colonial-era buildings including the High Court and State Central Library.
  • Vidhana Soudha: Karnataka's grand granite state legislature building, a Dravidian landmark especially striking when lit up on weekend evenings.

Local food

  • Masala Dosa: A crisp rice-and-lentil crepe filled with spiced potato — try the original at Mavalli Tiffin Room (MTR) or Vidyarthi Bhavan.
  • Bisi Bele Bath: A hot, tangy rice dish cooked with lentils, vegetables and a distinctive spice blend, a Karnataka comfort-food staple.
  • Mysore Pak: A rich, ghee-soaked gram-flour sweet that originated in the Mysore royal kitchens and is sold across the city's sweet shops.
  • Filter Coffee: Strong South Indian coffee brewed in a traditional metal filter and served in a tumbler-and-dabarah set, a daily ritual at local darshinis.
