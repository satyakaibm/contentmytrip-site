Chonburi Province, Thailand

Pattaya

Bangkok's easy beach escape — bold nightlife by night, island boat trips by day.

Best time to visitNovember to February
Ideal duration2–3 days
Nearest airportU-Tapao Rayong-Pattaya International Airport (about 35 km) or Bangkok Suvarnabhumi (about 110 km)
Budget₹25,000–₹55,000 per person for 3 days
Typebeach

Pattaya is a lively Thai beach resort city known for its nightlife, water sports, floating markets, and easy day trips to nearby islands like Koh Larn.

Experiences

  • Koh Larn day trip: Speedboat out to Koh Larn for a calmer beach day and snorkelling.
  • Water sports: Try parasailing, jet-skiing or banana boating along Jomtien and Pattaya beaches.
  • Walking Street nightlife: Experience Pattaya's famous strip of bars, clubs and street performances.
  • Floating market visit: Browse boats selling food and crafts at one of Pattaya's floating markets.

Attractions

  • Pattaya Beach & Walking Street: The city's main beachfront by day, transforming into a neon nightlife strip after dark.
  • Koh Larn (Coral Island): A quieter island a short speedboat ride away, known for clear water and water sports.
  • Sanctuary of Truth: An all-wood temple-like structure carved entirely in traditional Thai and Khmer style.
  • Nong Nooch Tropical Garden: A sprawling botanical garden with cultural shows and landscaped topiary.

Local food

  • Tom Yum Goong: Hot-and-sour shrimp soup, a Thai classic found at every seaside restaurant.
  • Pad Thai: Stir-fried noodles with tofu, egg, shrimp and peanuts.
  • Grilled seafood: Fresh catch grilled beachside along Jomtien Beach's evening stalls.
  • Mango Sticky Rice: Sweet coconut sticky rice served with ripe mango, Thailand's favourite dessert.
